What is Munjaane Coffee?

Munjaane Coffee is a South Indian filter coffee brand offering freshly packed coffee powder for your everyday cup.

What is the Ordinary blend?

Our Ordinary blend contains 60% coffee and 40% chicory for a bold and strong everyday filter coffee.
